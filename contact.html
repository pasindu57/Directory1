Frequently Asked Questions

Is BODIMA free to use?

Yes. Browsing, contacting owners and posting your property are all completely free. BODIMA never charges commissions.

What does "Verified" mean on a listing?

Only listings whose details (photos, price, address) have been checked by the BODIMA team carry the verified badge. Listings without the badge are still shown but marked unverified.

Why does my new listing show "Pending Approval"?

New submissions appear immediately in the directory but remain marked as pending until our team reviews them. This keeps the platform trustworthy for everyone.

How do I contact a property owner?

Open any listing and use the "WhatsApp Owner" or call button. All contact happens directly between you and the owner.
